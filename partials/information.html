What is Vexec?

Vexec is a fully anonymous social space. Unlike mainstream platforms, we don't ask for your real name, we don't link accounts to phone numbers, we don't track your activity across the web, and we don't store session data on our servers.

You operate under a display name of your choosing and gain access with a unique 20-character code that only you know. That code is your identity — there is no recovery, no reset, no backup. If you lose it, your account is gone.

Vexec was designed from the ground up around one principle: what you say should stand on its own. Without a name to attack, without a profile to judge, without a history to weaponize, conversation returns to what it was always meant to be — ideas and honesty.

How it works

Using Vexec is intentionally minimal. There are only three steps:

  1. Create your identity. Choose a display name and a username. Complete a human verification check to ensure you're not a bot.
  2. Save your access code. We generate a unique 20-character code. Write it down. Store it safely. This is the only way to log back in — we cannot recover it for you.
  3. Speak. Write whispers, comment on others, save what matters to you. Everything is anonymous by default.

There is no profile picture by default, no bio required, no verification of who you are. Your display name can be anything — it doesn't need to reflect reality at all.

If you lose your code, you lose your account. This is a feature, not a bug. Any recovery mechanism would require us to store identifying information — which would destroy the entire premise of Vexec.

The anonymity model

Most platforms claim to protect your privacy while quietly collecting everything about you. Vexec is built the other way around — we collect nothing, by design.

Specifically, Vexec does not store:

  • Your real name, phone number, email, or national ID.
  • Your IP address at any point.
  • Your approximate location or device fingerprint.
  • Session timestamps or activity logs.
  • Any link between your access code and your real identity.

This isn't a marketing claim — it's a technical reality. When you register, we don't collect identity data because there's nowhere for it to go. The only thing you have is your access code. Even if our infrastructure were breached, the attacker would find codes and content — nothing that leads to a person.

The trade-off is equally real: there is no password reset, no account recovery, no support ticket that can restore access. This is the cost of true anonymity, and we believe it's worth it.

What is not allowed

Freedom of speech on Vexec comes with responsibility. To keep this space safe for everyone, some categories of content are strictly forbidden. These are enforced seriously, and violations carry consequences up to permanent ban.

Fake news & scams

Publishing false or misleading information, or attempting to defraud users. Example: claiming a real company is going bankrupt without any evidence.

Violent & adult content

Obscene material, graphic violence, credible threats of harm, or anything related to child abuse. This is the highest severity category and results in permanent ban without warning.

Spam & disruption

Repeated messages, advertising, malicious links, or anything that degrades conversation. Automated posting via bots is included.

Toxicity & hate speech

Insulting, degrading, threatening, or attacking individuals or groups based on race, religion, gender, or sexual orientation.

Privacy violations (Doxxing)

Publishing someone's private information without consent — their real name, address, phone number, or workplace.

The full list — with definitions, examples, and specific consequences for each violation — is available in our Terms of Service.

Privacy & data

Vexec's privacy model is simple: we collect nothing, because we have nothing to collect. There's no email, no phone number, no real name — you never provide those to us.

Beyond registration data, Vexec also doesn't log:

  • Login times or session duration.
  • Which posts you read, liked, or scrolled past.
  • Your device type, browser, or OS.
  • Your IP address at any point in any log.
  • Referrer information from other websites.

The only data we hold is what you voluntarily publish: your display name, your content, and your interactions. Your access code is stored as your login credential — but it is not linked to your identity in any way we could reveal.

If we were legally compelled to hand over information, the only thing we could provide would be content you already made public. We couldn't reveal who you are, because we don't know.

How enforcement works

We are committed to protecting your freedom of speech — and equally firm against illegal and harmful content. Enforcement is proportional to severity and repeated behavior.

Severity Typical outcome
Low Content removed, warning issued. Repeated violations escalate to temporary restrictions.
Medium Temporary restrictions ranging from 24 hours to 7 days, depending on the rule and offense count.
High Permanent ban without prior warning. Applied immediately when content falls into the highest-severity categories.
Illegal In cases involving credible threats, child abuse, fraud, or terrorism, we may be legally required to cooperate with law enforcement — though we hold no identifying data to share.

Enforcement decisions are made by the Vexec moderation team. Every user has the ability to report content they believe violates these rules, using the report button available on every post.

Note: because we don't collect identifying data, even "cooperation with law enforcement" is limited to the content itself. We cannot turn over your IP, location, or identity because we never had them.

Our philosophy

Freedom of speech is not a slogan. It's a practice — one that only works when people feel safe enough to actually speak. Most places that claim to protect free expression quietly punish the people who use it, by tying their words back to their names.

Vexec exists to break that link. Here, your words travel without your identity. They stand or fall on their own merits. Nobody can dig through your history, cross-reference your other accounts, or find your employer and send them screenshots.

This doesn't mean consequences disappear. The rules in our Terms of Service are real, and enforcement is real. But those consequences attach to your access code — never to the person behind it.

If you're here to be honest, you're welcome. If you're here to hurt people, you'll be removed. That's the entire philosophy.
