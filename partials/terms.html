Terms of Service

By using Vexec you accept the rules below. They exist to keep this space safe for everyone. Violations carry consequences proportional to their severity and frequency — from a warning, to a temporary restriction, to permanent removal of your access code.

Last updated · September 2026 Applies worldwide Version 2.1

Vexec is an anonymous platform. We do not collect personal data, IP addresses, session timestamps, or anything that could identify you. This anonymity comes with a heightened responsibility — you are free to speak, but not to harm.

The rules below define where that line is drawn. Every account is bound by them the moment it is created. If you don't agree with any of these rules, do not use the platform.

01

Spam & Unwanted Advertising

Medium
Definition

Repeatedly sending the same message, promotional links, irrelevant content, or using automated tools to publish en masse. Spam degrades the experience for everyone and pushes real conversations out of view.

What counts as spam
  • Posting the same content more than five times within a short window.
  • Dropping promotional links, referral codes, or affiliate URLs.
  • Copy-pasting an identical reply across unrelated posts.
  • Mass-mentioning users who have no connection to your content.
  • Posting content unrelated to the platform's tone or intent.
  • Using bots, scripts, or third-party tools to auto-publish.
Examples Someone posts a link to an online store 50 times across different comment sections. Or a user publishes identical "crypto signal" posts every 30 seconds from multiple accounts.
Consequences
OffenseAction taken
1st offense All spam content removed + private warning to your account.
2nd offense 24-hour restriction — you cannot post, comment, or reply.
3rd offense Permanent ban of your access code. No recovery possible.
02

Fake News & Misinformation

Medium
Definition

Deliberately publishing false information, spreading unverified rumors, or distorting facts with the intent to deceive. This rule targets intentional deception, not honest mistakes or differences of opinion.

What counts as misinformation
  • Claiming a company, person, or institution has done something, with zero evidence.
  • Fabricating quotes, statistics, or events.
  • Sharing doctored screenshots, images, or audio as if they were real.
  • Spreading health or safety claims that could cause harm.
  • Circulating unverified "insider" financial claims that could affect markets.
  • Deliberately mislabeling opinion as fact.
Examples Claiming with no evidence that a legitimate company is going bankrupt, pushing readers to sell their shares. Or fabricating a quote from a public figure to support a political narrative.
Consequences
OffenseAction taken
1st offenseContent removed + formal warning.
2nd offense72-hour full restriction.
3rd offensePermanent ban.

Violent, Threatening or Adult Content

High
Definition

Any obscene material, graphic depiction of violence, credible threats to harm others, or content related to child abuse. This is the highest-severity category on Vexec — there is no second chance and no warning.

What counts
  • Threatening to hurt, kill, or stalk a specific person.
  • Sharing videos or images of graphic real-world violence.
  • Any sexual content — nudity, explicit material, or suggestive imagery.
  • Content sexualizing minors in any form.
  • Content promoting, praising, or providing instructions for violence.
  • Sharing material depicting self-inflicted harm.
Examples Posting "I know where you live and I'm coming for you" to a specific user. Or uploading a clip of real-world graphic violence. Or sharing any image that sexualizes a minor.
Consequences
OffenseAction taken
Any offense Immediate content removal.
Any offense Permanent ban of your access code — no prior warning.
Serious cases If content involves child abuse or credible threats to physical safety, we will report to law enforcement with whatever content we have. We store no identifying data, so this is limited to the content itself.

Scams & Fraud

High
Definition

Any attempt to deceive users into giving money, personal information, or access credentials. This includes financial scams, phishing, fake charities, romance scams, and any form of social engineering.

What counts as fraud
  • Claiming a "guaranteed investment" that turns out to be a Ponzi scheme.
  • Requesting crypto, gift cards, or wire transfers for fake services.
  • Pretending to be someone in distress to extract money.
  • Running fake charity drives or GoFundMe-style campaigns.
  • Selling goods or services that do not exist.
  • Phishing links designed to steal credentials.
Examples Claiming a "risk-free 300% return" opportunity and collecting money from users. Or posing as a stranded traveler needing urgent funds. Or offering to sell items that never ship.
Consequences
OffenseAction taken
Any offenseImmediate content removal.
Any offensePermanent ban of your access code.
Serious cases Reported to law enforcement when legally required.

Privacy Violation (Doxxing)

High
Definition

Publishing someone's private information — real name, address, phone number, workplace, financial details, or any data that could be used to locate or identify them — without their explicit consent.

What counts as doxxing
  • Posting someone's home address or phone number.
  • Revealing someone's real identity behind an anonymous account.
  • Sharing screenshots of private conversations without consent.
  • Publishing financial information (bank accounts, transactions).
  • Revealing someone's location in real time.
  • Encouraging others to find and expose a specific user's identity.
Examples Posting someone's home address or phone number without asking them. Or unmasking an anonymous user by revealing their real name based on personal knowledge.
Consequences
OffenseAction taken
Any offenseImmediate content removal.
Any offensePermanent ban of your access code.

Self-Harm & Suicide Content

High
Definition

Content that promotes, glorifies, or provides instructions for self-harm or suicide. Discussions of mental health struggles are welcome — promotion of self-harm is not.

The line we draw
  • Allowed: talking honestly about depression, anxiety, or difficult moments.
  • Allowed: asking for support or sharing your own recovery story.
  • Not allowed: graphic descriptions of methods.
  • Not allowed: encouraging others to harm themselves.
  • Not allowed: sharing images or videos of self-inflicted harm.
  • Not allowed: framing self-harm as a solution or a "way out".
Examples Writing "I've been struggling lately and don't know who to talk to" is welcome and will be met with care. Writing "here is how you do it" or "everyone should just give up" is not.
Consequences
OffenseAction taken
Any offenseContent removed immediately.
Any offensePermanent ban of your access code.
Support If you are struggling, please contact a local crisis line. You matter, and there is help.
